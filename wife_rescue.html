Field guide

How to play

You're navigating a fictional security building. Think first; then move.

1. Inspect the terminal. Press E next to the green terminal. Run help, then ls and cat patrol.txt. Windows CMD uses dir and type patrol.txt.

2. Plan your route. Guards and dogs repeat their patrol paths. Blue dots appear after reading patrol.txt. Camera cones are shown in red. Enter disable cam1 (use the IDs listed in cameras.txt) to disable one camera.

3. Progress carefully. Read mission.txt to find the fictional mission command. Use unlock lift on Floors 1–4, then stand on the gold exit tile and press E. On Floor 5, rescue your wife first, then use unlock roof. At the roof, reach the helicopter and press E.

Important: The 30-minute clock keeps running while the terminal is open. Guards pause while you type. A collision with a guard, dog, or active camera beam ends the run. Restart at any time to try a new strategy.
