Mission briefing · 05:00 available

Get her out. Get out together.

A mafia boss is holding your wife on the fifth floor. His helicopter is waiting on the roof. Sneak through the building, avoid guards, dogs, and cameras, open locked doors with E, rescue your wife, and escape together. The five-minute timer never stops.

Watch the patrolsGuards follow repeating routes. Learn when they turn and cross behind them.
Open locked doorsWalk close to a locked door and press E. No extra menus or tools are needed.
Avoid captureCameras can alert nearby guards, but only physical contact with a guard or dog can catch you.
Escape togetherOn Floor 5, enter through the open left-side passage or unlock the lower door. Press E beside your wife, then reach the rooftop helicopter.

Controls: Move with WASD or the arrow keys. Press E near a locked door, your wife, a stairwell, or the helicopter. On Floor 5, enter through the open left-side passage or unlock the lower door.
